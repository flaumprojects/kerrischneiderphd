Payment Information

Payment in the form of cash, check, and credit card is accepted.

Dr. Schneider is not an in-network provider for any medical insurance plans. However, Dr. Schneider can provide an itemized receipt upon request that can be submitted to the insurance company in the event the insurance plan has out of network benefits.

In addition, families can also seek reimbursement for Dr. Schneider’s services through their HSA/FSA plans as well as through the Step Up For Students: FES-UA Scholarship.

Please contact Dr. Schneider to discuss specific fees for service.
