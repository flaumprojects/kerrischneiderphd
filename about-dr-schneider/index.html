About Dr. Schneider

Dr. Kerri Schneider is a licensed clinical psychologist who has worked with children, adolescents and families for over twenty years. She is in private practice in Lake Worth, Florida, where she provides individual and family therapy, consultation, and psychological and neuropsychological assessment.

She works with a wide range of concerns, including attention and behavior problems, anxiety and mood disorders, learning disorders, autism spectrum disorders, parental divorce, peer and family relationships, and psychological factors in chronic illness. Her approach draws on cognitive-behavioral therapy, developmental theory and neuropsychology.

Dr. Schneider served for four years as Director of Psychological Training at the Center for Child Development, where she oversaw outpatient mental health services and psychology training programs. She has also provided developmental evaluations for children from birth to three years through the Early Steps Program in Palm Beach County. She lectures to community groups, professionals and school personnel, has presented at professional conferences, and has published in scientific journals.

Education and Training

  • Ph.D. in Clinical Psychology, University of South Florida
  • Internship in Clinical Child and Pediatric Psychology, University of Miami/Jackson Memorial Hospital
  • Two-year Postdoctoral Fellowship in Pediatric Psychology, Mailman Center for Child Development, University of Miami School of Medicine
